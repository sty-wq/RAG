在 multilingual tweet annotation study 中，约15%的推文被有意重复，交由同一标注者或两位不同标注者再次标注，用于计算 self-agreement 和 inter-annotator agreement，并估计标注一致性；这针对的是标注者一致性/标注不确定性。[1] 在 time-ordered Twitter sentiment evaluation 中，采用 blocked、stratified、10-fold cross-validation，并在所有实验中使用；相关讨论还提到通过移除训练集与测试集之间的相邻观测来制造间隔，以处理依赖观测问题；这针对的是时间有序数据中数据独立性被违反所带来的评估不确定性。[2][4]

### replay

In the multilingual tweet annotation study, about 15% of tweets were intentionally duplicated to be annotated twice, either by the same annotator or by two different annotators; from multiple annotations by the same annotator the authors computed self-agreement, and from multiple annotations by different annotators they computed inter-annotator agreement, with confidence intervals estimated by bootstrapping [1]. In the time-ordered Twitter sentiment evaluation work, the authors addressed the issue of dependent observations for blocked cross-validation by removing adjacent observations between the training and test sets, effectively creating a gap between the two [4]; they also note that they focus on longer-term time-dependence between the training and test sets and completely ignore finer-scale dependence at the level of individual tweets (e.g., retweets and replies) [5]. Separately, in the multilingual study's classification experiments, blocked, stratified, 10-fold cross-validation was applied in all experiments [2], and in its language datasets analyses the labeled sets were ordered by tweet post time, increased by 10,000 tweets at each stage, with 10-fold cross-validation performed after each stage [3].

评审：第一项正确（15%重复标注、self-agreement与inter-annotator agreement）。但第二项未给出时间有序评估的关键事实：138对in-sets/out-sets、在每个in-set上训练并在out-set上测样本外性能；X只讲移除相邻观测和忽略细粒度依赖，未回答held out了什么及对应评估不确定性，故比较错误。
